# Getting Started

A quick tour of LOTTO — the Line Order Transparency & Ticketing Organizer — and
where to go for each task. If you're new, start here.

## What LOTTO Does

LOTTO runs a fair, transparent ticket queue for events like a food pantry
distribution. Clients hold a numbered ticket; the system shows who is being
served now, who's next, and roughly how long the wait is. It also offers a
multilingual public board, an optional "what's in stock" inventory list when
the organization connects FEED, and a small arcade to keep waiting guests
entertained.

## The Three Surfaces

LOTTO has three main areas:

![The Staff Dashboard used to prepare and run a ticket queue](/help-screenshots/staff-dashboard.webp)

- **Staff Dashboard** (`/admin`) — where staff set the ticket range, call
  numbers, and manage the day. Staff sign in first at `/login` with the default
  Magic Link or a 6-digit Verification Code; an unauthenticated visit to
  `/admin` also prompts sign-in. Magic Links show a confirmation page before
  signing in so organizational email scanners cannot use them first. See
  [Staff Controls](02-staff-controls.md).

![The Public Board showing Now Serving, service details, and the drawing order](/help-screenshots/display-board.webp)

- **Public Board** (`/display`) — the big screen clients watch to see the number
  being served now. See [The Display Board](03-display-board.md).

![The phone-sized Arcade menu showing the available games](/help-screenshots/arcade.webp)

- **Arcade** (`/arcade`) — optional games for waiting guests. See
  [The Arcade](07-arcade.md).

Clients themselves usually open the home screen on their phone to look up their
own ticket. See [Tickets & the Queue](04-tickets-queue.md).

## A Typical Day

1. Open the **Staff Dashboard** and set today's ticket range and operating hours.
2. Put the **Public Board** up on a TV or large screen in the waiting area.
3. As you're ready for each person, **call the next number** from the dashboard.
4. Clients watch the board or their phone and come up when their number is called.
